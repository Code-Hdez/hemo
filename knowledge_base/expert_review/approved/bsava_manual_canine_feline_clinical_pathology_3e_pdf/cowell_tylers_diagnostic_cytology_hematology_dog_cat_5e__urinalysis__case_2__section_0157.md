# CASE 2

## CASE 2


9-year-old female neutered Domestic Shorthair cat.


The cat was missing for 6 months and returned with swelling of the right hindlimb. The right hindfoot was distorted and seeping pus. The right popliteal lymph node was enlarged and there were discrete subcu tan eous swellings over the right tarsus with fistulae discharging pus.

Amoxicillin/clavulanic acid MIC: 128 µ g/ml

Predicted mean concentration of amoxicillin/clavulanic acid in urine (dosage 25 mg/kg orally q8h) (Dowling, 2012): 201 µ g/ml

Cefalexin MIC: &gt;256 µ g/ml

Predicted mean concentration of cefalexin in urine (dosage 30 mg/kg orally q8h) (Dowling, 2012): 500 µ g/ml

There was concern over the likely efficacy of cefalexin and amoxicillin-clavulanic acid because of the high MIC relative to the predicted concentration of the antimicrobial in urine. For a 90-95% probability of efficacy, the mean urine concentration of an antimicrobial should be greater than four times the MIC concentration. This was not satisfied for either antimicrobial.
