# Erythrocytosis

## Erythrocytosis

Erythrocytosis (also referred to as polycythaemia) is chara c terized by an increase in the red cell count, haemoglobin concentration and PCV. It can be classified as relative or absolute. Absolute erythrocytosis is categorized as primary (true) or secondary. Relative erythrocytosis is caused by disturbances in fluid balance resulting in dehydration. The total red cell mass remains normal but the decrease in plasma volume results in an increase in the total plasma protein concentration.

Animals with erythrocytosis may have red mucous membranes and congested retinal blood vessels. The hyperviscosity may lead to polyuria and polydipsia (PU/ PD), weakness, ataxia and, in severe cases, seizures or visual disturbances.
