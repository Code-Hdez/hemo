# Monitoring after diagnosis

## Monitoring after diagnosis

Appropriate diagnostic techniques are required for monitoring dogs and cats during or after treatment. In some diseases, such as canine leishmaniosis, monitoring is also performed to check that a subclinically infected animal is not developing disease (Solano-Gallego et al. , 2009). The main diagnostic assays used for monitoring are quantitative serology and PCR.
