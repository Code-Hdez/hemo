# Bacterial meningoencephalomyelitis

## Bacterial meningoencephalomyelitis

This appears to be uncommon in dogs and cats. It is also characterized by marked pleocytosis (usually &gt;200 cells/ µ l, often &gt;500 cells/ µ l) and is frequently accompanied by elevated microprotein. Neutrophils predominate (&gt;70% of total nucleated cells) and may be degenerate. When intra­ cellular bacteria are seen, this provides strong support for the presence of sepsis (Figure 25.11). Bacteria are not seen in all cases and correlation with the results of bacterial culture is important.
