# HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THE TOTAL T4 RESULT?

## HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THE TOTAL T4 RESULT?

The total T4 result can be believed because this patient was recently shown to be TgAA negative. It would be very unlikely that TgAA (including T4 cross-reacting subsets) would start to appear after diagnosis and the initiation of therapy. On the face of it, the total T4 value seems reasonable; it is well inside the reference interval for healthy dogs.
