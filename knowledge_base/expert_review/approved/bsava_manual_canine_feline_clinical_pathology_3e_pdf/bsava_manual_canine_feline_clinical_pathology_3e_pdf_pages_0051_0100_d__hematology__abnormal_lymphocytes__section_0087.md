# Abnormal lymphocytes

## Abnormal lymphocytes

The presence of circulating abnormal cells is much more suggestive of lymphoproliferative disease than any change in lymphocyte count. The features associated with malig­ nancy are discussed in Chapter 21 and illustrated in Figure 5.15. The terms 'overspill leukaemia' and 'leukaemic phase of lymphoma' are sometimes used to describe increased or aberrant lymphoid cells in the circulation of patients with lymphoma; these terms are misleading, because these patients do not have leukaemia. In lymphoma, abnormal lymphoid cells on blood smear examination may reflect either escape of cells from neoplastic peripheral lymphoid tissue into the circulation (so­called 'overspill') or bone marrow involvement.
