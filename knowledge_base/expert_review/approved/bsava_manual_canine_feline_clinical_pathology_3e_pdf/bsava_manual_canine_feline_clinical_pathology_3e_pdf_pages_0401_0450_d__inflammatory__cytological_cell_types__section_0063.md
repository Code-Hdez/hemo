# Cytological cell types

## Cytological cell types

Cytology is used to classify the nature of the aspirated lesions into inflammatory, hyperplastic, neoplastic (benign or malignant) or response to tissue injury. The distinction among these categories is simple when the lesion consists of either inflammatory or tissue cells, but is more difficult when both inflammatory and tissue cells are aspirated.
