Biochemistry and haematology were run in­house. There was a mild elevation in urea (11 mmol/l; reference interval (RI) 6-10 mmol/l) and a mild elevation in alanine aminotransferase (ALT) (134 IU/l; RI 30-75 IU/l). There was a mildly elevated RBC count (10.16 x 10 12 /l; RI 5.5-10.0 x 10 12 /l) and marginal eosinophilia (1.8 x 10 9 /l; RI 0.0-1.5 x 10 9 /l).
