# Body cavity effusions

## Body cavity effusions


Body cavity effusions occur when there is abnormal accumulation of fluid in a body cavity. In dogs and cats, effusions commonly occur in the pleural, peritoneal or pericardial spaces. Clinical signs, such as dyspnoea, lethargy, exercise intolerance and abdominal distension, can be due to the presence of the effusion, the disease responsible for producing the effusion or both. Analysis of effusions is an important component of diagnosis: it can identify the pathological process responsible for the fluid accumulation and lead to a specific diagnosis, or it can indicate further investigative procedures which may be helpful.
