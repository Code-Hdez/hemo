# Degenerative changes

## Degenerative changes

Degenerative changes occur in neutrophils in the tissues rather than in the circulation, as a result of endotoxin exposure; they include nuclear swelling, chromatin disso­ ciation and nuclear eosinophilia (see Figure 5.3l). These changes are more often appreciated in tissue and fluid samples, and are only rarely seen in the circulation in bacteraemia septicaemia.
