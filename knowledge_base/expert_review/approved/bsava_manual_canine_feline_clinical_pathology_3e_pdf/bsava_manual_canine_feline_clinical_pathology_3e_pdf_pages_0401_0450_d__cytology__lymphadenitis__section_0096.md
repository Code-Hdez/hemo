# Lymphadenitis

## Lymphadenitis

Lymphadenitis is defined as inflammation of the lymph node. It is classified according to the prevalent inflam­ matory cell type. Lymphadenitis is usually accompanied by a reactive lymphoid population with predominantly small lymphocytes and with variably increased proportions of intermediate and large forms and plasma cells (see later).
