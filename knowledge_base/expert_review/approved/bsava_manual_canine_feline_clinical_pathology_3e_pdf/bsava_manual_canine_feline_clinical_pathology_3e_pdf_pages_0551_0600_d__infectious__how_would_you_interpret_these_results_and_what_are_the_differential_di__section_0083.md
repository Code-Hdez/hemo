## HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS AND WHAT ARE THE DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSES?

The histopathological findings are compatible with aplasia of the megakaryocytic, myeloid and erythroid cell lines.

The most likely differential diagnoses for pancytopenia in this case, based on the clinical history, clinical findings and clinicopathological abnormalities, are infectious causes (ehrlichiosis due to Ehrlichia canis , histoplasmosis or, less likely, leishmaniosis) or aplastic anaemia (e.g. due to oestrogen toxicity, an oestro genproducing tumour or drug-induced toxicity).
