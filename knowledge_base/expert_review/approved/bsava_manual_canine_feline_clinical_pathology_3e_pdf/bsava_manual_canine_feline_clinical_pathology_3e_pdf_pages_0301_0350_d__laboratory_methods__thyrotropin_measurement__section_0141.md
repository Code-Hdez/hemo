# Thyrotropin measurement

## Thyrotropin measurement

Species­specific chemiluminescent assays are widely available for measurement of canine TSH (cTSH). However, a species­specific feline assay is not yet avail­ able. It has been shown that, while not identical, feline TSH is more similar to cTSH than to human TSH (Rayalam et al ., 2006b). Thus, there has been growing interest in using the canine assays for assessment of feline TSH, despite other studies suggesting that the canine assay detects less than 40% of recombinant feline TSH (Rayalam et al ., 2006a).
