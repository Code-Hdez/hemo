# Method

## Method

- Invert the sample to mix it thoroughly
- The dipstick is introduced into the urine sample, withdrawn and the excess removed by tapping lightly against the edge of the container or, in the case of a small sample, applied to the strip with a pipette
- The dipstick is allowed to 'develop' for the required length of time
- The colour on each pad is compared with the reference tones on the kit packet
- The results are recorded, usually in a semi-quantitative fashion ('negative', 'trace', '1+', '2+', '3+', etc.)
