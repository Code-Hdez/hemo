## IS THERE ANY SPECIFIC CYTOLOGICAL FEATURE THAT MAY SUGGEST A THYROID GLAND ORIGIN?

Between the clusters of cells, there is variable amount of amorphous pink material, which is colloid. This is the material produced by thyroid follicular cells and contains the precursors of thyroid hormones.
