# Sample requirements

## Sample requirements

Taurine concentrations can be measured in either heparinized plasma or heparinized whole blood (Kramer et al. , 1995). In feline plasma samples, taurine concentrations decrease significantly in response to fasting; this is not the case for whole blood samples, which are therefore preferred in this species (Pion et al. , 1991; Heinze et al. , 2009). In dogs, heparinized plasma is most commonly used. Samples for both species should be acquired prior to the initiation of dietary taurine supplementation owing to rapid increases in circulating concentrations. There is some variation in the reference intervals for taurine given in the literature; the concentrations given in Figures 20.1 (Kramer et al. , 1995) and 20.2 (Heinze et al. , 2009) are intended as a guide and it is important to check with the laboratory performing the test.
