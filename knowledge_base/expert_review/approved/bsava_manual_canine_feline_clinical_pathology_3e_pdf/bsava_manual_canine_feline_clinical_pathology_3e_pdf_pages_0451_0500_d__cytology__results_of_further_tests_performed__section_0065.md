# RESULTS OF FURTHER TESTS PERFORMED

## RESULTS OF FURTHER TESTS PERFORMED

Haematology, biochemistry and urinalysis are unremark­ able. Radiographs of both carpi reveal moderate soft tissue swelling but no evidence of bone lytic or prolifer­ ative lesions. Abdominal ultrasonography reveals a mild enlargement of the medial iliac lymph nodes. Cytology of the fine­needle aspirates from the prescapular and pop­ liteal lymph nodes is compatible with reactive lymphoid hyperplasia. Serology for Borrelia spp., Ehrlichia spp. and Anaplasma spp. is negative. Serology for Leishmania spp. is positive with a low titre, indicating prior exposure but not diagnostic of active infection. PCR was then performed on lymph node aspirates and was negative, making leishmaniosis unlikely.
