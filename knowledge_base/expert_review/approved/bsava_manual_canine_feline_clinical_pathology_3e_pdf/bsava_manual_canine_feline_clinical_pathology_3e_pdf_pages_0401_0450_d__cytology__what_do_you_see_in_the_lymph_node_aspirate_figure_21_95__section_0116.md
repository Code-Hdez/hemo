# WHAT DO YOU SEE IN THE LYMPH NODE ASPIRATE (FIGURE 21.95)?

## WHAT DO YOU SEE IN THE LYMPH NODE ASPIRATE (FIGURE 21.95)?

There is a mixed lymphoid cell population with a pre­ dominance of small lymphocytes and a few larger forms. In this sample there are also low numbers of macro­ phages (indicated by asterisks). Low numbers of fungal hyphae were also seen in the lymph node (in the centre of the image). This indicates macrophagic lymphadenitis caused by a fungal infection.
