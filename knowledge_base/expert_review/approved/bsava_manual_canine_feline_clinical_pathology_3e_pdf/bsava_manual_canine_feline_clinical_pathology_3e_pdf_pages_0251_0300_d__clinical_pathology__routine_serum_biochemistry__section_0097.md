# Routine serum biochemistry

## Routine serum biochemistry

A full panel of routine biochemical tests is recommended in all cases with chronic or severe acute GI signs although, in most instances, the main purpose of performing such tests is to exclude dysfunction of other body systems including renal, hepatic and endocrine disorders.
