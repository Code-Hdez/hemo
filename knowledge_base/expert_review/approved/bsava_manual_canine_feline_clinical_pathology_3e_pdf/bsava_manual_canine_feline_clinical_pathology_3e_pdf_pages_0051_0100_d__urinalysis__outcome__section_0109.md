# OUTCOME

## OUTCOME

Bone marrow aspirates were of low cellularity but revealed relative granulocyte hyperplasia, normal matu­ ration of erythroid and myeloid series and megakaryo­ cyte hypoplasia. There was no evidence of a neoplastic infiltrate. Later core biopsy specimens confirmed there was no evidence of myelofibrosis or myelonecrosis.

Abdominal ultrasonography revealed heptomegaly with hypoechoic change, and splenomegaly with normal echogenicity. Liver aspirate revealed only normal hepat­ ocytes. Urinalysis was unremarkable apart from an equivocal urine specific gravity (USG = 1.023) and there was no proteinuria.
