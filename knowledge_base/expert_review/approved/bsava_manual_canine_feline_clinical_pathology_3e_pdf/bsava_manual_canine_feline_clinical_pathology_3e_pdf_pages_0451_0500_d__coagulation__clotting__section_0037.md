# Clotting

## Clotting

Under normal conditions joint fluid is devoid of coagulation factors. Joint fluid becomes a gel under static conditions, mimicking the formation of a clot. However, if shaken, it becomes thin and less viscous. This property is called thixotropy . True clots can form if there is blood contamina­ tion or in cases of inflammation, when there is leakage of proteins including fibrinogen and other coagulation factors from synovial vessels.
