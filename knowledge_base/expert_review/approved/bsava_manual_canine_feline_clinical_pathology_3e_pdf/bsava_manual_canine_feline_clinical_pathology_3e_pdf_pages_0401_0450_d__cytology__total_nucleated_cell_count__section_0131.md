# Total nucleated cell count

## Total nucleated cell count

The combination of TP and TNCC is used to classify fluids as transudate, protein­poor, transudate, protein­rich, or exudate (Figure 22.3). TNCC can be performed using a haematology cell counter or a haemocytometer. The TNCC includes the mesothelial cells, macrophages, white blood cells and any other nucleated cells in the effusion.
