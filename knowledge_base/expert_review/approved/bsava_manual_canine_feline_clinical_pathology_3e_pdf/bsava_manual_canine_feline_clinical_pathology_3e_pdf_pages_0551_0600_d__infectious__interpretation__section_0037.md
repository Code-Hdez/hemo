# INTERPRETATION

## INTERPRETATION

The coronavirus antibody titre in the serum is high (&gt;640 is considered high). Antibodies in young kittens can be maternally derived but not normally after 14 weeks, and are not likely to be so high, together suggesting they are endogenously derived in this case.

Several factors consistently point to a diagnosis of FIP, including the high FCoV serum antibody, high AGP and a sterile exudate with an A:G ratio less than 0.81. Most definitive is the high viral load in the ascitic fluid. The key to diagnosis in this case was recognizing and obtaining a sample of the ascitic fluid.
