# Eosinophilic lymphadenitis

## Eosinophilic lymphadenitis

This is diagnosed when eosinophils make up more than 3% of all nucleated cells (Figure 21.79; Raskin and Meyer, 2010). Primary differentials include type I hypersensitivity, parasitic infections, feline eosinophilic skin diseases, hyper­ eosinophilic syndrome and paraneoplastic syndromes (usually associated with mast cell tumour, T­cell lymphoma and rarely carcinomas).
