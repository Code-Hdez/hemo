# NT-proBNP in canine heart disease NT-proBNP in degenerative mitral valve disease

## NT-proBNP in canine heart disease NT-proBNP in degenerative mitral valve disease

The diagnosis of degenerative mitral valve disease (DMVD) in an older, small or medium-sized dog can be made on the basis of careful auscultation and therefore does not require NT-proBNP measurement. However, increasing circulating NT-proBNP concentrations are associated with increasing severity of DMVD, and with more rapid disease progression. Dogs with NT-proBNP concentrations &gt;740 pmol/l have been shown to have decreased survival times, independent of the eventual cause of death (Moonarmart et al. , 2010). In dogs with compensated DMVD, plasma NT-proBNP concentrations &gt;1500 pmol/l indicate that the risk of onset of congestive heart failure (CHF) in the next 6 months is high (Reynolds et al. , 2012).
