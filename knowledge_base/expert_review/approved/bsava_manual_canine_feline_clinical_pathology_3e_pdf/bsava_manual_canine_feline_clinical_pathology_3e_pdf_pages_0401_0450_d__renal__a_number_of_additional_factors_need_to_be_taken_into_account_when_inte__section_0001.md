# A number of additional factors need to be taken into account when interpreting the results of plasma NT-proBNP tests

A number of additional factors need to be taken into account when interpreting the results of plasma NT-proBNP tests:

- NT-proBNP is increased in patients with compromised renal function
- NT-proBNP is increased in cats with systemic hypertension
- NT-proBNP is increased in dogs with pulmonary hypertension.

As with any clinical test, careful patient selection will improve the clinical usefulness of NT-proBNP measurements (Oyama et al ., 2013).
