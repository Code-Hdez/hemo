# Exfoliative cytology

## Exfoliative cytology

This relies on the spontaneous exfoliation of cells into fluids or on to swabs. Typical examples of exfoliative cytology are:

- CSF, urine and fluid cytology (pleural, abdominal, pericardial and synovial fluids): cytology smears are prepared from the spun sediment or using a cytospin centrifuge, or for cellular samples a direct smear or line concentration smear is made
- Vaginal and ear cytology: this is performed by rolling a cotton bud on the surface to be examined and then rolling this along the surface of a slide.
