# Virus neutralization

## Virus neutralization

Specific antibody in the test sample is used to neutralize a set amount of a laboratory virus. Neutralization of the virus is detected by the lack of development of cpe in cell cul­ ture. Virus neutralization tests are relatively laborious and, for many viral infections, have been superseded by newer and quicker methods. A good example of their remaining use is the measurement of anti­rabies antibody titres following vaccination as part of the PETS travel scheme, where the antibody titre has to reach a certain level for the animal to be classified as responding to vaccination, thus allowing travel from certain non­EU countries.
