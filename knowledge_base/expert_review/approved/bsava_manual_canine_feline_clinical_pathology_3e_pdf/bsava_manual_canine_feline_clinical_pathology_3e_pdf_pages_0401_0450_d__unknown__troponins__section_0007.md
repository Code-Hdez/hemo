# Troponins

## Troponins

Cardiac troponins I, T and C are involved in the regulation of myocyte contraction. Unlike BNP, they are intracellular proteins that are only found in the circulation if cardiomyocytes have been damaged or destroyed. They are, therefore, considered markers of cardiac injury. It is pos sible to use assays designed for human samples to measure cardiac troponins in dogs and cats. The use of cardiac troponin I (cTnI) has been most extensively described.
