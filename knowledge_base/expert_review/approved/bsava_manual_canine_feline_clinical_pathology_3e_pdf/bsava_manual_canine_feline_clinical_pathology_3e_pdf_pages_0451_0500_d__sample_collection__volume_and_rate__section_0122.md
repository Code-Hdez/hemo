# Volume and rate

## Volume and rate

- Approximately 1 ml of CSF per 5 kg bodyweight can be collected safely.
- Collection of approximately 1 ml per 30 seconds is safe.
- The volume of CSF collected should not exceed 4-5 ml from a dog, 0.5-1.0 ml from an adult cat, or 10-20 drops from a kitten or puppy.
