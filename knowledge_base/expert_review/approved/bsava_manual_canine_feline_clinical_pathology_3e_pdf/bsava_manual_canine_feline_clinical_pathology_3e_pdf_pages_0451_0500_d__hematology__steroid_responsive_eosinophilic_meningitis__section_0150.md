# Steroid-responsive eosinophilic meningitis

## Steroid-responsive eosinophilic meningitis

Steroid­responsive eosinophilic meningitis has been reported in dogs and cats. This has been hypothesized to be the result of an allergic (type I hypersensitivity) reaction. Slight to marked pleocytosis may be present, but usually &gt;80% of the nucleated cells are eosinophils. Protozoal, parasitic or fungal infections need to be ruled out. Golden Retrievers may be predisposed to this condition. There is usually a good response to glucocorticoid treatment.
