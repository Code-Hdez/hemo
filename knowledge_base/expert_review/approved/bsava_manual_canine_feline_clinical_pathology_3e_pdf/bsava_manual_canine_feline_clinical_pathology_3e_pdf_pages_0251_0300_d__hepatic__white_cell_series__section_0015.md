# White cell series

## White cell series

There are no white blood cell (WBC) changes pathognomonic for hepatic disease. The total WBC count may be increased and an inflammatory leucogram seen in acute infectious disease (e.g. leptospirosis) and sometimes in severe chronic inflammatory hepatopathies and neoplastic liver diseases with necrosis of tumour nodules (see Chapter 5).
