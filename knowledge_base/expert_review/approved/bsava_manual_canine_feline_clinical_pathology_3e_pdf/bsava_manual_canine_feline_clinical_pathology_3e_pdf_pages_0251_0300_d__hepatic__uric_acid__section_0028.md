# Uric acid

## Uric acid

Uric acid is an excretory metabolite of hepatic purine degradation, but increased serum uric acid concentration is rarely used as a marker of hepatic dysfunction. It has a sensitivity and specificity of 65 and 59%, respectively (Hill et al ., 2011). In contrast, the identification of urate crystalluria can suggest the presence of hepatic dysfunction (see Figure 12.12). However, uric acid also accumulates in dogs such as Dalmatians with inherited abnormalities of purine metabolism.
