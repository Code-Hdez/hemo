# Neutrophilic lymphadenitis

## Neutrophilic lymphadenitis

This is characterized by the presence of more than 5% neutrophils and is usually associated with bacterial, neoplastic or immune­mediated conditions (Raskin and Meyer, 2010).

Normal lymph node in a dog. (a) Tissue section. Cortical area containing lymphoid nodules composed mostly of small lymphocytes. (Haematoxylin and eosin stain; original magnification X40). (b) The fine-needle aspirate from the same lymph node is characterized by the prevalence of small lymphocytes. (Wright-Giemsa stain; original magnification X500) 21.78
