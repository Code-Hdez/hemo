# Laboratory testing

## Laboratory testing

Routine haematology and serum biochemistry, as well as urinalysis (including urine culture, since urinary tract infection may be a cause or consequence of poor glycaemic control) are useful in ruling out other causes of weight loss, polyuria or polydipsia and highlighting abnormalities which may be present as a result of other diseases, e.g. hypercalcaemia, neutrophilia and azotaemia.
