# Necrotizing encephalitis in small-breed dogs

## Necrotizing encephalitis in small-breed dogs

Necrotizing encephalitis in small­breed dogs has been primarily reported in young (&lt;4 years old) Pugs and Maltese and Yorkshire Terriers. This condition has been hypothesized to be an immune­mediated disease with autoantibodies directed against astrocytes. Multifocal to massive necrosis and non­suppurative inflammation of the cerebrum and meninges occurs and may be fatal. Seizures, depression and ataxia are common presenting signs. Usually there is a moderate pleocytosis, sometimes with &gt;200 cells/ µ l, with lymphocytes predominating (usually &gt;70%). Microprotein is often &gt;0.5 g/l.
