# Sample collection

## Sample collection

There are three methods of sample collection: free catch; catheterization of the bladder; and cystocentesis. Voided samples can be useful for basic tests: they are straightforward and cheap to collect, but do have their limitations because of the potential for contamination. Cystocentesis samples avoid contamination from the genital tract and are often less traumatic for the patient than catheterization, so are the ideal sample type in most cases. Morning samples tend to be more concentrated, which increases the chances of detecting any abnormalities, hence they are preferred where possible.
