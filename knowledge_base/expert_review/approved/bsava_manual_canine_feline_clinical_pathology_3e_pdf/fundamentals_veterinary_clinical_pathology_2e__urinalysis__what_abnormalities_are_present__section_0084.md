## WHAT ABNORMALITIES ARE PRESENT?

The cat was moderately hypernatraemic and hyperchloraemic and also had mildly elevated alanine aminotransferase (ALT). The specific gravity of the urine was hyposthenuric, which is unusual in a cat; their urine is usually well into the hypersthenuric range.
