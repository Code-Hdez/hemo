# Canine and feline rheumatoid arthritis

## Canine and feline rheumatoid arthritis

- Radiographic evidence of bony destruction
- Seropositivity for rheumatoid factor
- Compatible cytological findings in synovial fluid (suppurative inflammation or mixed mononuclear and suppurative with high cell count)
- Stiffness after inactivity
- Swelling and/or pain in at least one joint
- Involvement of at least another joint within 3 months
- Symmetrical joint swelling
- Compatible histological findings in synovial membrane
