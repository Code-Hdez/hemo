# Volume

## Volume

The volume of joint fluid usually correlates with the distension of the joint capsule, which may be estimated subjectively by physical examination. The amount of fluid that can be obtained by arthrocentesis in healthy patients ranges from one drop to 0.25 ml in cats and one drop to 1 ml in dogs, depending upon the joint sampled and the animal's size. Higher volumes may be collected in diseases characterized by joint effusion.
