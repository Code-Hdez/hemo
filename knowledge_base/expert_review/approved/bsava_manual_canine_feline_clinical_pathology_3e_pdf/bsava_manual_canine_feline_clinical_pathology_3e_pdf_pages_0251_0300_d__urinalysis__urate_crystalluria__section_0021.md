# Urate crystalluria

## Urate crystalluria

Abnormal uric acid metabolism and hyperammonaemia in liver disease can result in precipitation of ammonium biurate crystals in the urine. Occasionally, massive crystalluria causes obvious brown turbidity (Figure 12.12a) but, most often, characteristic crystals are found by microscopic examination of urine sediment. The crystals resemble mites or thorn-apples (Figure 12.12b). Ammonium biurate crystals can be found on serial urinalyses in approximately two-thirds of dogs with congenital PSS. Amorphous urate and uric acid can also be found on sediment examination.
