# Cytology of infectious agents

## Cytology of infectious agents

Infectious agents are frequently encountered during the cytological examination of canine and feline samples. They can be broadly classified into bacterial, protozoal, fungal and parasitic infections. The scope of this chapter is to describe the morphological features of the most common aetiological agents found in Europe, and especially in the UK. For the clinical implications of these agents, the reader should refer to specific textbooks.
