# Specialized diagnostic tests

## Specialized diagnostic tests

Numerous tests have been developed in an attempt to investigate GI diseases non-invasively. However, many of these specialized tests are not available commercially, many have not been properly validated and many are only applicable to referral and/or research institutions, rather than general practice. What follows is a summary of the main tests developed, and their indications. These tests are not recommended for routine use.
