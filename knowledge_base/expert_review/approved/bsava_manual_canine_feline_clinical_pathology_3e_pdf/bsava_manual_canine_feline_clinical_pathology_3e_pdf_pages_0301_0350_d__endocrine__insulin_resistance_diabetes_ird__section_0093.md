# Insulin resistance diabetes (IRD)

## Insulin resistance diabetes (IRD)

Primary IRD usually results from antagonism of insulin function by other hormones:

- Dioestrous/gestational diabetes
- Secondary to other endocrine disorders:
- Hyperadrenocorticism
- Acromegaly
- Iatrogenic:
- Synthetic glucocorticoids
- Synthetic progestagens
- Glucose intolerance associated with obesity may contribute to insulin resistance but is not a primary cause of diabetes in dogs
