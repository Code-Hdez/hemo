# Canine viruses

## Canine viruses

Diagnosis of disease associated with viral infections is generally more straightforward in the dog than in the cat because canine viruses tend not have complex carrier states, and canine vaccines appear to be much more efficient in blocking infection and disease. As such, vacci­ nation history is an important part of the diagnostic work­ up for canine viral infections.
