# HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS?

## HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS?

Pyuria, an increase in epithelial cells, bacteriuria and mild haematuria all provide evidence of infection of the urinary tract or prostate gland. The pure heavy growth of E. coli and presence of inflammatory sediment suggests this is a clinically significant organism rather than contamination of the urine with bacteria. When the urine is contaminated a mixed growth is often identified.
