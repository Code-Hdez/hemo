# Infectious disease titres

## Infectious disease titres

Myositis can be caused by the protozoal organisms Neospora caninum (dogs), Toxoplasma gondii (dogs and cats), Leishmania infantum (dogs), Sarcocystis spp. (dogs) and Trypanosoma cruzi (dogs). Less common causes of myositis include Hepatozoon canis and H. americanum , feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV), Ehrlichia canis , Leptospira australis , L. icterohaemorrhagiae , and clostridial infections. Many of these diseases are geographically specific, and rarely encountered. However, if a diagnosis of myositis is made on muscle biopsy, antibodies to protozoa should be measured and additional infectious diseases should be considered depending on the clinical picture (see Chapters 28 and 29).
