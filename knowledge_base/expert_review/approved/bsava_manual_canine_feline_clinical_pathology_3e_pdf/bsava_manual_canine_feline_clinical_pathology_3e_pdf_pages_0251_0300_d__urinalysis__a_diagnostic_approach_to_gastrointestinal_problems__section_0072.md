# A diagnostic approach to gastrointestinal problems

## A diagnostic approach to gastrointestinal problems

There is a battery of tests available for the investigation of GI diseases, although many lack sensitivity and/or specificity. Thus, the usual diagnostic approach is first to eliminate diseases not involving the GI tract. It is important to take a history and to perform a physical examination. See the BSAVA Manual of Canine and Feline Gastoenterology for further information.
