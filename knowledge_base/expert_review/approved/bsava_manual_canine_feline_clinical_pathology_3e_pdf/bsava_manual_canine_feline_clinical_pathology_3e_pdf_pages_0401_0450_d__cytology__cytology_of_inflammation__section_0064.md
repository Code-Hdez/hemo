# Cytology of inflammation

## Cytology of inflammation

As a general rule, a lesion is classified as inflammatory when all or most of the intact cells are inflammatory cells. Once inflammation has been recognized, the next step is to subclassify the process on the basis of the predominant cell type (e.g. neutrophilic, macrophagic, eosinophilic inflammation) because this provides important clues to the underlying aetiology.
