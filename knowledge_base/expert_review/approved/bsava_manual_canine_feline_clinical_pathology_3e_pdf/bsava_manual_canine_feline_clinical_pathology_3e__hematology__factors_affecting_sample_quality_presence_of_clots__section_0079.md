# Factors affecting sample quality Presence of clots

## Factors affecting sample quality Presence of clots

Samples containing clots will have a falsely low platelet count (marked effect) and falsely low leucocyte count (mild to moderate effect, depending on the size of the clot). The red cell count and analyser HCT are falsely lowered although the spun PCV may be falsely increased or decreased. Prior to any analysis the sample should be checked for clots. This is best achieved by using a small wooden stick (an 'orange stick'), which is wiped around the inside of the tube and then removed and examined. Any clots in the sample should be scooped up by the stick (Figure 3.1).

A blood clot in an EDTA sample is detected by wiping a wooden stick around the inner surface of the tube. Clotted samples should be discarded. 3.1
