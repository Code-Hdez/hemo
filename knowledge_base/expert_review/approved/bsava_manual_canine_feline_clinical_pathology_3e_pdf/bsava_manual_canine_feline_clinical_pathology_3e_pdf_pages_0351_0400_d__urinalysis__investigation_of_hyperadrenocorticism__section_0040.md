# Investigation of hyperadrenocorticism

## Investigation of hyperadrenocorticism

The process of investigating and confirming canine HAC is summarized in Figure 18.4. Before performing any labor­ atory tests for HAC it is important to obtain an accurate history and perform a thorough clinical examination (Herrtage and Ramsey, 2012).

Full blood and urine profiles are obtained to:

- Identify any non­specific indicators of the disease
- Exclude other causes of polyuria/polydipsia (PU/PD), polyphagia, etc.
- Identify intercurrent disease that might affect subsequent therapy.
