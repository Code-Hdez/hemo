# Marked neutrophilic inflammation

## Marked neutrophilic inflammation

Marked neutrophilic inflammation (&gt;50% neutrophils, with pleocytosis, often with increased microprotein) can be seen with bacterial meningitis and severe viral encepha­ litis, including FIP. It may also occur with acute steroid­ responsive meningitis-arteritis (also known as meningitis of unknown origin), following myelography, with trauma, haemorrhage, acquired hydrocephalus or with neoplasia, especially meningioma.
