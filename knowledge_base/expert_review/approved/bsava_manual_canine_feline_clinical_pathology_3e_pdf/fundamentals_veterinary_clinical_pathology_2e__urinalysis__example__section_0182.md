# Example

## Example

Mix 1 ml sample with 1 ml water

If the diluted sample measures 1.030

0.030 × 2 = 0.060

The SG is 1.060

The SG can increase slightly with large amounts of glucose or protein in the urine. The dogma that refrac to meters specially calibrated for feline urine must be used, has recently been debunked; indeed, felinespecific refractometers may underestimate SG in cats (Tvedten et al ., 2015).


Regularly 'zero' the refractometer with distilled water
