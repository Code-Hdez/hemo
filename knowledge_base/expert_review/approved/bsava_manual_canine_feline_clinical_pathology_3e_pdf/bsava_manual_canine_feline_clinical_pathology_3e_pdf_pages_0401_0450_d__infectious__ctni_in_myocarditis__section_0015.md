# cTnI in myocarditis

## cTnI in myocarditis

cTnI is increased as a result of myocardial injury secondary to inflammation and necrosis. Underlying causes include:

- Physical insults (e.g. trauma)
- Chemical insults (e.g. doxorubicin toxicity)
- Infectious causes (e.g. parvovirus, Toxoplasma gondii , Neospora caninum , Borrelia burgdorferi , Trypanosoma cruzi , Leishmania spp.).

Appropriate aberrant ancillary tests are indicated if the history is suggestive of an infectious myocarditis.
