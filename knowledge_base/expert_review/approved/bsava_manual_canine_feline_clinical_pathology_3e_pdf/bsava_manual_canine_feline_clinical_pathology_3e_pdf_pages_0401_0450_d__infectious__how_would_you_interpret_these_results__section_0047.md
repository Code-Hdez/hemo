## HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS?

The results of the BAL are consistent with a bacterial bronchopneumonia or bacterial bronchitis. The presence of intracellular bacteria suggests that the bacteria seen are not contaminants introduced during sample acquisition.
