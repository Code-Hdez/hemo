# Excess utilization of glucose

## Excess utilization of glucose

- Sepsis
- Neoplasia
- Pregnancy
- Seizure activity
- Exercise/working dog hypoglycaemia
- In vitro utilization/laboratory error
- Sample aging with in vitro metabolism of glucose when sample separation is delayed
- Leucocytosis (may only be relevant in cases of delayed sample separation)
- Polycythemia (may only be relevant in cases of delayed sample separation)
