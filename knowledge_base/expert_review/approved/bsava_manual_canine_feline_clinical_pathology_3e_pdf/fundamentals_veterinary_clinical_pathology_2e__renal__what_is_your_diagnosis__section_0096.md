# WHAT IS YOUR DIAGNOSIS?

## WHAT IS YOUR DIAGNOSIS?

Vitamin D intoxication with psoriasis cream (calcipotriol), with renal failure.


Calcipotriol is a synthetic vitamin D substance and is not detected using the routine assays for the two forms of vitamin D. This dog was severely hypercalcaemic but was also significantly azotaemic.
