# Cytology

## Cytology

Direct smears should be prepared immediately after sample collection and submitted along with the fluid. Slides should be kept away from sources of cold to avoid artefacts due to freezing (Figure 23.9). At the laboratory additional slides will be prepared, including direct and concentrated preparations, and all slides will be routinely stained using Romanowsky­type stains, air­dried and examined.

Guidelines for the examination of direct smears of synovial fluid are summarized in Figure 23.10.
