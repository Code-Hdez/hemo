# Protozoal and parasitic infections

## Protozoal and parasitic infections

The most common protozoal infections are toxoplasmosis (Figure 21.26), neosporosis, cytauxzoonosis and leish­ maniosis (Figure 21.27). These infectious agents most commonly elicit a mixed but predominantly macrophagic inflammatory response.

Examples of parasites that may be found on cytological preparations include Dirofilaria immitis (Figure 21.28a) and D. repens (Figure 21.28b), Angiostrongylus (Figure 21.29) and Aelurostrongylus spp. The inflammation caused by these parasites is variable but is usually mixed, with or without an eosinophilic component.
