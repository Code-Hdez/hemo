# Granulomatous meningoencephalitis

## Granulomatous meningoencephalitis

GME is most often seen in young to middle­aged, small and medium­sized dogs, especially of toy or terrier breeds, and occurs more often in females. Clinical signs include fever, ataxia, tetraparesis, cervical hyperaesthesia and seizures. The CSF may have slight to moderate lympho­ cytic inflammation, mixed cell pleocytosis (Figure 25.9) and/or occasionally neutrophilic pleocytosis. The majority of cases have nucleated cell counts &gt;100 cells/ µ l. Micro­ protein levels are variable.
