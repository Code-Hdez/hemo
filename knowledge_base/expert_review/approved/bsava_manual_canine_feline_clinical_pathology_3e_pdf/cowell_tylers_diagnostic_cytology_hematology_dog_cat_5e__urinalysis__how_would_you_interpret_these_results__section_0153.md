# HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS?

## HOW WOULD YOU INTERPRET THESE RESULTS?

Increased numbers of WBCs in the urine sediment are consistent with urogenital tract inflammation (see earlier section on Urinary tract infection).

A bacterial count of 6 x 10 3 cfu/ml of E. coli in a catheterized urine sample from a dog is considered equivocal in respect of urinary tract infection (see Figure 27.24).

The E. coli isolate is multidrug resistant. This includes resistance to a third-generation cephalosporin (cefovecin) and fluoroquinolones (enrofloxacin and marbofloxacin), raising suspicion that this could be an extended-spectrum beta-lactamase-producing coliform (ESBLPC). This was confirmed by the VITEK ® 2 analyser ESBL test.
