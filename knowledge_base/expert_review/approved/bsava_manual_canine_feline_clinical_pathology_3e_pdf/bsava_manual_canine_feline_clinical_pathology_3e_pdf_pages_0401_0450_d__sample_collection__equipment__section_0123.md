# Equipment

## Equipment

Equipment for collection of body cavity effusions includes:

- Clippers
- Surgical scrub and alcohol spray
- Local anaesthetic (optional)
- Sterile needle, butterfly needle, or over­the­needle catheter
- Sterile syringe
- Extension tube and three­way tap
- Scalpel blade and suture (for over­the­needle catheter only)
- Gloves
- Kidney dish or measuring jug (if collecting large volume of fluid for therapeutic reasons)
- Three sample tubes (ethylenediamine tetra­acetic acid (EDTA, plain, plain sterile).
