# Prothrombotic Properties of Endothelium

## Prothrombotic Properties of Endothelium

Endothelial cells store VWF in granules called Weibel Palade Bodies. This protein has important roles in FVIII biology, and in mediating platelet adhesion and aggregation (Chapter 81 ). Endothelial cells express a specifi c G protein coupled receptor for thrombin. Thrombin binding to this receptor initiates a number of procoagulant and proinfl ammatory events. Intracellular signaling initiates endothelial secretion of VWF and P - selectin from Weibel - Palade bodies, nitric oxide (NO) release, and phospholipase A 2 activation that results in platelet activating factor (PAF) production. Activated endothelial cells express intracellular adhesion molecule 1, generate interleukin 1, and secrete both plasminogen activators and PAI - 1.
