# Corticosteroid - induced Heterophilia

## Corticosteroid - induced Heterophilia

Corticosteroid - induced heterophilia is observed sporadically in diseased or severely stressed birds and is the result of corticosterone release from the adrenal cortex. An example of developing heterophilia in response to forced confi nement has been reported in captive herring gulls. 2,7 Corticosteroid - induced heterophilia is observed more frequently with exogenous corticosteroid administration or injection of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH), which stimulates endogenous corticosterone release. The phenomenon is dosage and route dependent. 1 Developing or concurrent lymphopenia separates this response from physiologic leukocytosis.
