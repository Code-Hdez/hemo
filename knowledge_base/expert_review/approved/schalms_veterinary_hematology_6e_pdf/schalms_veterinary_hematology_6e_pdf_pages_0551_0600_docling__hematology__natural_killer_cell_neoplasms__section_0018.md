# Natural Killer Cell Neoplasms

## Natural Killer Cell Neoplasms

Natural killer cells are incompletely characterized in domestic animals other than the cow because there is a paucity of useful reagents that defi ne their phenotype in humans and mice. Until now, a diagnosis of NK leukemia or lymphoma has been based on absence of T or B cell markers, and undetectable clonal rearrangements of lymphocyte antigen receptors. Yet, cells with morphologic characteristics and functions of NK cells can be isolated from canine blood, 82 and the recent characterization of canine and feline CD56 may help to confi rm the incidence of NK malignancies in these species. 179
