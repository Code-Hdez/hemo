# Basophilia

## Basophilia

A basophilia is recognized by an increased absolute basophil count ( &gt; 200 basophils/ µ L) and generally occurs with a concomitant eosinophilia. Some causes of basophilia in cats include allergic respiratory conditions, heartworm disease, eosinophilic granuloma complex, basophilic leukemia, myeloid leukemia, mast cell neoplasia and polycythemia vera. 3,9,18
