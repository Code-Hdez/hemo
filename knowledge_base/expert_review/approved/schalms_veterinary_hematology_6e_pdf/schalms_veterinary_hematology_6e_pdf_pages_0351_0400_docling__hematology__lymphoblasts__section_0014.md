# Lymphoblasts

## Lymphoblasts

Lymphoblasts are large lymphoid cells that are observed frequently in disseminated lymphoma and lymphoblastic leukemia (Fig. 48.15 ). Lymphoblasts have a large nucleus with vesicular chromatin and prominent nucleoli. The cytoplasm is abundant and has a deep blue hue. 36,39
