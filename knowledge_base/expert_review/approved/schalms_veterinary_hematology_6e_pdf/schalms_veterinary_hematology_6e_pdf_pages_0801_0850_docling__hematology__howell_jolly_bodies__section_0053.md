# Howell - Jolly Bodies

## Howell - Jolly Bodies

Howell - Jolly bodies are nuclear remnants that occasionally can be seen in equine peripheral blood fi lms in health (Fig. 106.1 A). They are small, round, purple inclusions found in discocytes. Increased numbers of Howell - Jolly bodies can be seen with increased eryth- ropoiesis and with decreased or compromised splenic function.

( B ) Six light - staining platelets. ( C ) Two segmented neutrophils, one small lymphocyte, one eosinophil, erythrocytes and platelets. ( D ) Three lymphocytes and erythrocytes; small lymphocyte that contains cytoplasmic granules (left), small lymphocyte with scant cytoplasm (center), small lymphocyte with slightly more cytoplasm (right). ( E ) Segmented neutrophil, basophil, erythrocytes and platelets. ( F ) Small lymphocyte (with azurophilic granules), monocyte and erythrocytes.
