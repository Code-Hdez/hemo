# Methods of Blood Group Detection

## Methods of Blood Group Detection

Hemolytic and agglutination techniques are used to assay blood group factors. The choice of procedure is largely determined by the characteristics of the RBCs of each species. Blood grouping assays for cattle, sheep, goat, llama and alpaca are hemolytic tests because the RBCs of these species are not prone to agglutination. Horse and pig blood groups factors are assayed by saline agglutination or by hemolytic tests. Some pig factors require an antiglobulin test (Coombs ' test) or addition of dextran to facilitate agglutination and improve scoring of reactions.
