# BLOOD BANKING - INTRODUCTION

## BLOOD BANKING - INTRODUCTION

The advent of large - scale veterinary blood banks has made the practice of high - quality transfusion medicine possible by increasing availability of blood components. Strict adherence to blood collection, processing, and storage guidelines is essential to providing safe and effective transfusions. This chapter details the currently accepted blend of human protocol and veterinary modifi cations where necessary for the collection, processing, and storage of canine and feline blood components.
