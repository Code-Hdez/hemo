# Pure Red Cell Aplasia Associated with an Immune Response to Recombinant Human Erythropoietin

## Pure Red Cell Aplasia Associated with an Immune Response to Recombinant Human Erythropoietin

Dogs frequently develop PRCA 4 - 16 weeks after initiation of treatment with recombinant human erythropoietin. 19 The anemia occurs because dogs develop an immune response to the recombinant protein. These antibodies cross - neutralize endogenous canine erythropoietin resulting in profound anemia. The onset of anemia is typically 2 - 3 months after initiation of therapy and dogs usually recover spontaneously 3 - 11 weeks after discontinuing treatment.
