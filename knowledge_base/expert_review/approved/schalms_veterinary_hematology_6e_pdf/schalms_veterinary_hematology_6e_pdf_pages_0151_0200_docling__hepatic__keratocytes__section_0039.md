# Keratocytes

## Keratocytes

Keratocytes are spiculated RBCs that frequently have only 1 - 2 spicules and indicate RBC fragmentation (Fig. 23.11 ); thus, the RBC appears to have ' horns ' (kerat is the Latin term for horn). The formation of keratocytes has been associated with microvascular injury, especially when there is deposition of fi brin strands. The opposing sides of the damaged RBC may fuse leading to formation of a blister. Bursting of the blister is thought to result in progression to a keratocyte. 22 Keratocytes have been reported in cats with liver disease 9 and in dogs and cats with chronic doxorubicin administration and hemangiosarcoma.
