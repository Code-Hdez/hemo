# Transplantation

## Transplantation

The transplantation procedure for HSCs, regardless of source, is similar. Because HSCs home to marrow, there is no need for homotopic transplantation. Grafts are typically administered intravenously at a dose ranging from 10 7 - 10 9 nucleated cells/kg body mass in canine and feline BMTs. Grafts enriched for CD34+ HSCs have been administered in dogs at doses of 10 6 - 10 7 CD34+ cells/kg, similar to the ' megadose ' HSCTs in humans. 28
