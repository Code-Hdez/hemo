# Immunodefi ciency

## Immunodefi ciency

Modifi cation of the immune system by viral proteins can enhance carcinogenesis through several mechanisms including disruption of tumor surveillance, atypical stimulation of the immune system, and alteration of the microenvironment (see Chapter 55 ). The association of lentivirus - induced immunodefi ciency and cancer has been well established in several systems such as HIV infection and the development of Kaposi sarcomas and B cell lymphomas.
