# PLATELET AGGREGATION

## PLATELET AGGREGATION

Platelet aggregation is the historical ' gold standard ' for testing platelet function defects, although this position is now being challenged by novel methods. The original platelet aggregation method in platelet - rich plasma (PRP) was described in 1962 by Born, and consisted of an absorptiometer with experiments performed at room temperature. 1 Platelet aggregation is currently being performed in PRP at 37 ° C using a spectrophotometer, or in whole - blood using an electrical impedance method. The aggregation pattern is classically evaluated in response to addition of an exogenous platelet agonist.
