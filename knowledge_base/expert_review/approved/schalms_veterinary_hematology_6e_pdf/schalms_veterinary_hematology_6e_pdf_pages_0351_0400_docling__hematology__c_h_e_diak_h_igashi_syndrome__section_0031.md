# C h é diak - H igashi Syndrome

## C h é diak - H igashi Syndrome

This syndrome occurs in Persian cats (see Chapter 42 ). 15,16 Circulating neutrophils and lymphocytes contain single to multiple, pink to reddish, intracytoplasmic granules (Fig. 49.6 ). Eosinophilic granules also may be enlarged.

FIGURE 49.5 Blood smear from a cat with mucopolysaccharidosis type VI. ( A ) Three neutrophils contain granular, purple, intracytoplasmic inclusions. ( B ) A neutrophil with intracytoplasmic inclusions and a basophil with dark blue - black granules. Wright stain. (Courtesy of Oklahoma State University, College of Veterinary Medicine teaching fi les.)


Cytoplasmic vacuolization of neutrophils can be observed with drug toxicities including high doses of chloramphenicol or phenylbutazone. Also, cholesteryl ester storage disease is associated with subtle cytoplasmic vacuolation of neutrophils, lymphocytes, and monocytes on fresh blood smears. 28 Neutrophil cytoplasmic granulation is also seen after storage of blood in EDTA.


A neutrophil granulation anomaly has been identifi ed in Birman cats (see Chapter 42 ). The anomaly is characterized by the presence of azurophilic cytoplasmic granules that resemble toxic granulation. 11 The anomaly is inherited as an autosomal recessive trait and is innocuous.

FIGURE 49.6 Blood smear from a cat with Ch é diak - Higashi syndrome. ( A ) The neutrophils have large, pink cytoplasmic lysosomes. ( B ) The basophils have large, round, lavender granules. Wright stain. (Courtesy of Oklahoma State University, College of Veterinary Medicine teaching fi les.)
