# Staining Procedure for Cytologic Preparations

## Staining Procedure for Cytologic Preparations

Wright - Giemsa or May - Gruenwald - Giemsa (MGG) staining provides optimal contrast for bone marrow cytology preparations. Rodent bone marrow requires slightly longer staining time with Giemsa staining compared with MGG stain for the best differentiation between nucleated RBCs and lymphocytes. The Giemsa stain needs to be freshly prepared just before use and care should be taken to maintain a pH of 6.8 for the phosphate buffer.
