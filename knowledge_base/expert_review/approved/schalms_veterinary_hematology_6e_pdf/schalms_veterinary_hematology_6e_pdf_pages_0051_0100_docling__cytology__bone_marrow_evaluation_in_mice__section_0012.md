# Bone Marrow Evaluation in Mice

## Bone Marrow Evaluation in Mice

Direct examination of bone marrow complements information gained from assessment of blood. The technique of bone marrow collection and analysis in mice is described in Chapter 132 . In fetal mice, which lack developed medullary hematopoiesis, cytologic examination of liver imprints for hematopoietic precursors is useful in assessing early hematopoietic function.
