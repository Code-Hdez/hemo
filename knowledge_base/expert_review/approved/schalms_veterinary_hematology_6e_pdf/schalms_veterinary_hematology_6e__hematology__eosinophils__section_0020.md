# Eosinophils

## Eosinophils

Eosinophils are slightly larger than neutrophils at 25 30 µ m in diameter. The red - orange cytoplasmic granules are typically small and round, with an electron - dense appearance via electron microscopy. 2 The granules frequently exhibit polar or bipolar clustering as they form aggregates on one or both sides of the nucleus. 2
