# Clinical Signs

## Clinical Signs

Clinical signs in cats with primary and secondary IMHA are primarily attributable to the anemia and consist mostly of lethargy and inappetence. Some cats also suffer from vomition, pica, constipation or obstipation, and polydipsia. 32,44,46,56 On physical examination, cats have mucous membrane pallor and are usually afebrile, with heart murmurs secondary to anemia. Some cats have mild organomegaly (mostly affecting the spleen) or icterus. 32,44 Cats with secondary IMHA may display additional clinical signs related to the underlying primary disease.
