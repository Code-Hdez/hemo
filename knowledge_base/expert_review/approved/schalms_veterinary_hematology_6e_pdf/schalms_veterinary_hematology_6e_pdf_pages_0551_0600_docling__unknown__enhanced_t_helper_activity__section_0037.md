# Enhanced T - Helper Activity

## Enhanced T - Helper Activity

Approaches to modify the T cell response to cancer have been attempted in dogs for a variety of cancers or to demonstrate proof - of - principle for strategies designed to enhance immune recognition of pan - tumor associated antigens associated with canine melanoma and mammary cancer. 5 Application of such strategies to hematopoietic cancers is in a more formative stage; however, successful antigen loading of canine CD40 activated B cells has been demonstrated. 21 Specifi c T cell responses were detected to model antigens and the intent is to apply this technology to spontaneous canine lymphoma.
