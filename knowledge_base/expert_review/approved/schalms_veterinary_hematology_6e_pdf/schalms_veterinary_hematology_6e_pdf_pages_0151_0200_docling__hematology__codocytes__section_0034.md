# Codocytes

## Codocytes

Codocytes (also termed target cells) resemble a bull ' s eye (Fig. 23.7 ). They have a central area of hemoglobin surrounded by a pale rim with hemoglobin at the periphery of the cell. Similar to acanthocytes, codocytes have an increased amount of cholesterol resulting in an overall increase in the surface area of the erythrocyte membrane. Codocytes are often observed in patients with iron defi ciency, cholestatic liver disease and post splenectomy; however, codocytes are also frequent fi ndings in regenerative anemias.
