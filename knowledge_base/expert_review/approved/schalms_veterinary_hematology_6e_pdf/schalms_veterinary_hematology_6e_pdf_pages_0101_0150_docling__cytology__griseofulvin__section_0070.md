# Griseofulvin

## Griseofulvin

Griseofulvin is a fungistatic antibiotic used for treatment of mycotic diseases. 33 Neutropenia or panleukopenia occur as an idiosyncratic reaction to griseofulvin in cats. 33,53 In most cases, this occurs several weeks after initiation of griseofulvin treatment. 33 The bone marrow is typically hypoplastic. The majority of cats recover after treatment is discontinued.
