# Phenobarbital/Primidone

## Phenobarbital/Primidone

Phenobarbital and primidone therapy have been associated with several types of hematologic dyscrasias in dogs. Because primidone is metabolized to phenobarbital, the mechanism of toxicity may be similar for both drugs. 41 The mildest hematologic disorder is neutropenia and thrombocytopenia. 37,59,65 The presence of granulocyte hyperplasia in bone marrow indicates that the neutropenia is due to destruction of mature granulo- cytes. A more frequent ADR is myelonecrosis or myelofi brosis (Fig. 16.3 ). 65,71 Dogs with myelonecrosis are frequently bicytopenic or pancytopenic. Bone marrow is characterized by multifocal areas of coagulation - type necrosis with variable degrees of myelofi brosis present. 71 Dogs with myelofi brosis, without concurrent necrosis, frequently have a severe non - regenerative anemia. The myelofi brosis may represent the chronic stage of myelonecrosis.
