# Mast Cell Tumors

## Mast Cell Tumors

Siamese cats are predisposed to mast cell tumors compared with other breeds. 19 In an assessment of 41 cats with mast cell tumors published in 2006, young Siamese cats were shown to be more likely to present with a military histiocytic form of cutaneous mast cell tumors. 16 It appears that visceral mast cell tumors (majority in the spleen) are more common in cats. Currently, there is no known association of infectious disease with mast cell tumor development.
