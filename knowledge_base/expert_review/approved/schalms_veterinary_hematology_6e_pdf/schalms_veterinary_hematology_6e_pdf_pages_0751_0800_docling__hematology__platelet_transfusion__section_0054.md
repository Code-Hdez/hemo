# PLATELET TRANSFUSION

## PLATELET TRANSFUSION

Platelet transfusion is indicated for the control of bleeding due to severe thrombocytopenia or thrombopathia. Platelet transfusion has not been as widely used in veterinary medicine as red blood cell (RBC) and plasma transfusion, and most reports concern platelet transfusion associated with experimental hematopoietic stem cell transplantation in dogs. However, with the use of more aggressive anticancer therapy and the increased willingness to treat complex hematologic disorders, there is an increasing need for platelet - rich blood products. These products include fresh whole blood (FWB), platelet - rich plasma (PRP) and platelet concentrate (PC). The latter may be prepared by centrifugation of FWB or by plateletpheresis, the selective removal of platelets from the donor ' s blood via an automated cell separator with return of RBCs and plasma to the donor.
