# LEUKOCYTES

## LEUKOCYTES

Reptilian leukocytes frequently present a diagnostic dilemma because morphology varies, at times signifi cantly, between reptilian species. This can lead to misclassifi cation of reptilian cells. For example, the eosinophils of the green sea turtle ( Chelonia mydas ) have two morphologically distinct eosinophils, 58 and have been morphologically misclassifi ed as neutrophils. 1 Reptilian leukocytes are typically classifi ed as heterophils, lymphocytes, eosinophils, basophils, and monocytes/azurophils (Fig. 127.2 ). This terminology will be used here.
