# Decreases in Platelet Count

## Decreases in Platelet Count

Decreased PLT counts are frequently associated with diffi cult venipuncture or PLT aggregation subsequent to inadequate blood coagulation, especially in dogs and rodents. Compound - related decreases in PLT counts can also result from impaired production or accelerated destruction or utilization. Test - material that reduces erythroid and myeloid production frequently inhibits PLT production as well. An example of hematology data interpretation is provided in Tables 13.1 and 13.2 .
