# Bone Marrow Collection in Llamas

## Bone Marrow Collection in Llamas

The sternum is the preferred site for bone marrow collection in llamas. 1 Adult llamas are sedated with xylazine and can be placed in a chute or in left lateral recumbency. The 3rd or 4th sternebra is located by following the rib to its articulation. The site is aseptically prepared and anesthetized as described for cattle. A 15 gauge × 5 cm styletted Jamshidi or Illinois sternal biopsy needle with the needle guard removed can be used for aspiration. An 11 gauge × 10 cm Jamshidi needle can be used for core biopsies.
