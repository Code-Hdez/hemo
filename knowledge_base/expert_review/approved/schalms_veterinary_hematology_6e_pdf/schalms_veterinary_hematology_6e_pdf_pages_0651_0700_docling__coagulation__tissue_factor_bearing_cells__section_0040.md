# Tissue Factor Bearing Cells

## Tissue Factor Bearing Cells

As previously discussed, exposure of TF on a TF - bearing cell is the physiologically relevant trigger for in vivo coagulation. Tissue factor - bearing cells are primarily outside the vasculature, particularly in the adventitia, skin, mucous membranes, and organ capsules. Under pathologic conditions leukocytes, endothelial cells, and neoplastic cells may also express TF.
