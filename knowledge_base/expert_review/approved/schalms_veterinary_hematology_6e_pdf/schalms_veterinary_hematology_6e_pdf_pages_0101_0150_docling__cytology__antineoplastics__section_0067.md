# Antineoplastics

## Antineoplastics

Neutropenia and thrombocytopenia are frequently dose - limiting factors in chemotherapy. 16 As in other species, rapidly proliferating progenitor and proliferative cells in bone marrow are susceptible to destruction by chemotherapeutic agents. Beyond cytopenias, dysplastic hematopoietic cells and atypical mitotic fi gures (i.e. secondary dysmyelopoiesis) are frequently found in bone marrow. 67 Doxorubicin administration to cats has been associated with poikilocytosis including the presence of ovalocytes, echinocytes, schistocytes, and keratocytes. 45
