# Granular Lymphocytes or Natural Killer Cells

## Granular Lymphocytes or Natural Killer Cells

Granular lymphocytes or natural killer cells are null lymphoid cells that are seen infrequently in blood smears (Fig. 48.14 ). They have a few distinct azurophilic granules that tend to cluster at the nuclear margin or indentation. 36
