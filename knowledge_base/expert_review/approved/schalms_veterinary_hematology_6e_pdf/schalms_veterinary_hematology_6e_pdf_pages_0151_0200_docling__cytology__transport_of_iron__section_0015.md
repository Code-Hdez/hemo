# Transport of Iron

## Transport of Iron

T ransport of iron from one compartment or tissue to another through the circulation (e.g. enterocytes to bone marrow cells) is accomplished by the carrier protein transferrin. Serum transferrin is a soluble 78 kDa protein that binds one or two iron atoms in their ferric (3 + ) form.
