# Increases in Red Blood Cell Parameters

## Increases in Red Blood Cell Parameters

Relative erythrocytosis is frequently observed with subclinical or clinical dehydration in preclinical studies, especially in animals with decreased food consumption or in rodents with prolonged fasting. With relative erythocytosis, increases in RBC parameters (RBC count, Hgb, Hct) most often correlate with increases in total protein levels or albumin and/or urea. Absolute erythrocytosis resulting from a persistent increase in RBC mass and associated expansion of blood volume is rarely observed in toxicity studies, but may be seen when testing recombinant erythropoietin or other cytokines, or when the test article infl uences the production of erythropoietin.
