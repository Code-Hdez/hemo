# Bone Marrow Collection in Cats

## Bone Marrow Collection in Cats

Bone marrow collection in cats is from the iliac crest, the proximal humerus, and, occasionally from the trochanteric fossa of the femur. In cats, general anesthesia is frequently required. To obtain bone marrow aspirates from the iliac crest, the cat is positioned in sternal recumbency with hind limbs tucked alongside the body. Straight positioning is important to get bone marrow from the iliac crest. The overall collection technique is comparable to the procedures described in dogs.
