# Other Properties of Endothelial Cells

## Other Properties of Endothelial Cells

Endothelial cells synthesize NO in response to a variety of stimuli including thrombin, histamine, ATP, bradykinin, and acetylcholine. Nitric oxide inhibits platelet activation and causes vasodilation. Thrombin and other stimuli also induce endothelial cell synthesis of prostacyclin (PGI 2 ), a platelet inhibitor that also maintains vascular relaxation. 8
