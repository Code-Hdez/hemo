# Reticulocytes

## Reticulocytes

Reticulocytes are released from the canine marrow in a cyclic manner with a periodicity of approximately 14 days. 30 Reference intervals will vary between laboratories based on methodology employed and reference population used; however, adult dogs generally have less than 1.0% reticulocytes in peripheral blood or an absolute reticulocyte count of less than 70,000/ µ L. Neonatal animals have signifi cantly higher reticulocyte counts, a manifestation of increased erythropoiesis needed to keep up with a rapidly expanding vascular volume. Reticulocyte counts of up to approximately 10% can be seen in dogs during the fi rst 2 months of life. 10,11 This number decreases to adult levels by approximately 5 - 6 months of age.
