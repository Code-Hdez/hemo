# HEMATOLOGY CHANGES

## HEMATOLOGY CHANGES

Minor effects of test - article effects on clinical pathology results are frequently observed. Clinical terms (e.g. anemia, leukocytosis) should be avoided unless major effects are observed and there is a deliberate attempt to underline an important effect. Compound - related effects are usually described as increases or decreases.
