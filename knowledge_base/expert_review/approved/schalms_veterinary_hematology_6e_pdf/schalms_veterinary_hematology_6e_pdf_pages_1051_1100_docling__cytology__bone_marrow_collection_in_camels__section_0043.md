# Bone Marrow Collection in Camels

## Bone Marrow Collection in Camels

Collection of bone marrow has been described from the dorsal rib of dromedary camels. 17 Camels were sedated by intravenous administration of xylazine. A site over the 5th through 8th dorsal rib was aseptically prepared and anesthetized as described for cattle. The bone marrow needle and details of the technique were not described.
