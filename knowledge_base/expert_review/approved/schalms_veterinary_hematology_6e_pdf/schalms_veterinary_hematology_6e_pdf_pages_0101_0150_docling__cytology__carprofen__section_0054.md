## Carprofen

Coagulation - type myelonecrosis has been reported in bone marrow from several dogs treated with therapeutic doses of carprofen. 65 Another dog was reported to have a neutrophilic dermatitis, vasculitis, immune - mediated hemolytic anemia, and thrombocytopenia associated with carprofen adminstration. 43
