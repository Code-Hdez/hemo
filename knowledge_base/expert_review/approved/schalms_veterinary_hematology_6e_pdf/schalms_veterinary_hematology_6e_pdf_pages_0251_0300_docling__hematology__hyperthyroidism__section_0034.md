# Hyperthyroidism

## Hyperthyroidism

To our knowledge, only one study has looked at the association between hyperthyroidism and development of HBs in cats. 6 In this study 15 out of 21 cats with hyperthyroidism had an increased percentage of HBs and their presence usually did not result in overt anemia.
