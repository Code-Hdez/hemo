## Canines

Small signet - ring piroplasms have been identifi ed in RBCs from Spanish dogs with fever and hemolytic anemia. 61 This organism has been provisionally named Theileria annae . Babesia conradae , a small piroplasm identifi ed in California dogs with hemolytic anemia, has been shown to be more similar to Theileria species than to other Babesia species based on genetic studies. Thus, the specifi c nomenclature for many of these small piroplasms will likely be revised.
