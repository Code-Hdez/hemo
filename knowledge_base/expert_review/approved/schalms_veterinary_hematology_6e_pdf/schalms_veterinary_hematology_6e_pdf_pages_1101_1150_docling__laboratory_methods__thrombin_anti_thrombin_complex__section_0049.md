# Thrombin Anti - Thrombin Complex

## Thrombin Anti - Thrombin Complex

The thrombin anti - thrombin (TAT) complex is an indicator of the inhibition of thrombin by its natural inhibitor AT. In the ELISA for TAT, a thrombin specifi c capture antibody binds the TAT complex. After a washing step, a second antibody detects the AT moiety of the complex in a color reaction. The values are reported in µ g/L or ng/mL. 151 The assay has been applied in horses 142,144,155 , dogs 84 and in other laboratory animal species. 122
