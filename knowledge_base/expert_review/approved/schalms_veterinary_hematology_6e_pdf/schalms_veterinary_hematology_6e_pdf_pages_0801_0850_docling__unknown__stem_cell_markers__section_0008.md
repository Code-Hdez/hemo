# Stem Cell Markers

## Stem Cell Markers

The HSC phenotype has been defi ned as lineage marker negative (Lin -), Sca - 1+, CD34+, c - kit+, Hoechst low, and rhodamine low in mice and baboons. 5,61,75 Canine CD34+/rhodamine low cells are c - kit+. 64 CD34 expression was considered the hallmark of the ' true ' HSC for transplantation until it was demonstrated that CD34 -HSCs were capable of successful long - term engraftment. 26,43 Data suggest CD34 expression refl ects activation state and is reversibly expressed by HSCs; thus, HSCs may be variably CD34+ or CD34 -. 55 In dogs, humans, baboons, pigs, cattle, and mice, CD34+Lin -HSCs are capable of long - term multi - lineage hematopoiesis. 5,12,34,53,61 Anti - canine and anti - bovine CD34 monoclonal antibodies are available for HSC enrichment. 39,53 The feline CD34 molecule has recently been cloned, but the long - term repopulating ability of feline CD34+Lin -cells has not yet been characterized. 77
