# Feline Blood Components

## Feline Blood Components

Feline FWB is placed in one bag, and the tubing between this bag and an empty satellite bag is obstructed by folding and securing with a rubber band or a hand sealer clamp. Due to the relatively small volume of blood being separated, the collection/satellite bags may be bound (via rubber band or tape) to a 500 mL bag of saline to prevent the bags from collapsing during centrifugation. The blood bags are centrifuged at 3000 × g for 10 minutes at 4 ° C, and separation is continued as for dogs. The pRBCs may be stored without addition of AS (in which case 10 mL 0.9% sodium chloride is added just prior to administration to the patient), or 10 mL AS may be added to pRBCs prior to storage.

Feline PRP is rarely prepared due to a limited clinical need, as well as technical challenges; FWB is more likely to be administered to thrombocytopenic or thrombopathic cats with severe bleeding. However, preparation of feline PRP has been described, with the transfused platelets exhibiting in vivo effi cacy. 8,9
