# Anti - Infl ammatory Response

## Anti - Infl ammatory Response

Monocytes and lymphocytes in many humans and dogs with septic and non - septic infl ammatory diseases have decreased function, as determined by detecting decreased MHC class II expression, suggesting impaired immune responsiveness. 45 When stimulated with various agonists, these monocytes tend to produce predominately anti - infl ammatory cytokines. This state is thought to represent a compensatory anti - infl ammatory response. The purpose of this anti - infl ammatory response is to prevent an uncontrolled systemic infl ammatory response that could lead to respiratory distress syndrome and multiple organ dysfunction.
