# Hemolytic Anemia with Increased Erythrocyte Osmotic Fragility in Cats

## Hemolytic Anemia with Increased Erythrocyte Osmotic Fragility in Cats

A hereditary RBC defect is suspected in Abyssinian and Somali cats with Coombs ' negative hemolytic anemia. 38,39 The affected cats exhibited recurrent anemia (hematocrit, 15 - 25%); severe splenomegaly with extra medullary hemopoiesis, hemosiderosis, congestion, and lymphoid hyperplasia; loss of body mass; macrocytosis; and a few stomatocytes. The anemia was variably regenerative. The osmotic fragility of their RBCs was markedly increased. Splenectomy partially corrected the anemia and prevented hemolytic crises, but long term survival remains unknown.
