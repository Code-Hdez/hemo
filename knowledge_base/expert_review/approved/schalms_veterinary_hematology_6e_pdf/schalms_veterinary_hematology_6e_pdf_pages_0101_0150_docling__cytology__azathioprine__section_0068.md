# Azathioprine

## Azathioprine

Cats appear to be highly sensitive to azathioprine induced bone marrow suppression. Of fi ve cats given 2.2 mg/kg of azathioprine on alternate days, four developed multiple cytopenia. 7 Azathioprine - induced bone marrow suppression in humans has been associated with homozygosity for the gene for thiopurine methlytransferase. 7 This enzyme plays an important role in the catabolism of azathioprine.
