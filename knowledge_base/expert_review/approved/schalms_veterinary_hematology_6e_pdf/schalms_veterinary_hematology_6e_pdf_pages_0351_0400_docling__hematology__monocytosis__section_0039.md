# Monocytosis

## Monocytosis

An absolute monocyte count &gt; 850 cells/ µ L is considered a monocytosis. 9,18 Monocytosis is a nonspecifi c fi nding that has been reported to occur in approximately 11% of the leukograms of hospitalized cats. 18 Monocytosis is not a characteristic feature of the stress leukogram in cats as it is in dogs. Monocytosis occurs in many conditions, including both acute and chronic infl ammation, tissue destruction, and neutrophilia. Some of the reported causes of a monocytosis include trauma - related injuries, suppuration, necrosis, pyogranulomatous infl ammation, hemolysis, hemorrhage, malignancy, and immune - mediated disorders.
