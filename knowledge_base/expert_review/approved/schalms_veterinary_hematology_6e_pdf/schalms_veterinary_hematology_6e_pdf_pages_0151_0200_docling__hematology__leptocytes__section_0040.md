# Leptocytes

## Leptocytes

Leptocytes are thin RBCs that can fold and take on the shape of other poikilocytes such as codocytes, knizo- cytes (central fold giving the appearance of a central bar of hemoglobin) or stomatocytes. Leptocytes can be observed in iron defi ciency and cholestatic liver disease. 22
