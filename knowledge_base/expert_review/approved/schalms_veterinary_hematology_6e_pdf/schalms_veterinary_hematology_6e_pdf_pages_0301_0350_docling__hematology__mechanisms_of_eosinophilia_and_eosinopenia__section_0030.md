# MECHANISMS OF EOSINOPHILIA AND EOSINOPENIA

## MECHANISMS OF EOSINOPHILIA AND EOSINOPENIA

Factors infl uencing the number of eosinophils in the circulation include: diurnal variation related to cortisol levels, age, exercise, and exposure to environmental stimuli. Eosinophils are enumerated in several ways. The least accurate is the differential white blood cell (WBC) count performed on a stained blood fi lm, but this method is useful in most situations. Eosinophil counting chambers also can be used, but automated methods that stain peroxidase provide the most accurate counts. Eosinophil peroxidase is not detected by this method in all animal species, especially cats. The reference interval for eosinophils varies among species and geographic regions. Eosinophilia is not always present in the face of eosinophilic infl ammation in tissues where eosinophil survival is increased. Specifi c causes of eosinophilia and eosinopenia are discussed in Chapters 46 - 50 .
