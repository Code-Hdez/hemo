# Monocytes

## Monocytes

Monocytes in NHPs are larger than the lymphocytes and may appear in two forms. The fi rst type of monocyte has a broader band of cytoplasm than is observed in the lymphocytes, with a mottled gray - blue color with a foamy appearance or with discrete vacuoles (Fig. 110.12 A). The second type of monocyte, a transitional form, has a large oval nucleus devoid of depressions, and its cytoplasm appears darker adjacent to the nucleus and often has delicate pale reddish granulation. In general, monocyte counts are low in healthy NHPs, typically ranging from 0% to 4% of the total WBC count.
