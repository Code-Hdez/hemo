# Clinical Thrombotic Disorders

## Clinical Thrombotic Disorders

Thrombosis is recognized as a common complication of many common diseases in animals. Observational studies include immune - mediated hemolytic anemia (IMHA), hyperadrenocorticism, cardiac disease, and neoplastic and protein losing disorders as primary disease conditions often associated with systemic thrombosis and pulmonary thromboembolism (PTE) (see Chapter 87 ). Although hemostatic abnormalities are often described, the specifi c causal factors of thrombosis in animals with these conditions remain ill defi ned. Regardless of underlying mechanisms, thrombosis increases morbidity and mortality beyond that of the primary disease and often prompts consideration of antithrombotic therapy.
