# CELL BASED MODEL OF THROMBIN GENERATION

## CELL BASED MODEL OF THROMBIN GENERATION

Our new understanding of hemostasis incorporates the role of cells. This model suggests that in vivo coagulation occurs in distinct overlapping phases that require the participation of two different cell types: TF - bearing cells and platelets.
