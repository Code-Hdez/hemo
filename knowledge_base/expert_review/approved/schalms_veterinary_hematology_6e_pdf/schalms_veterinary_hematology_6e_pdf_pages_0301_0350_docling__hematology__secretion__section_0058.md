# Secretion

## Secretion

Macrophages contain a variety of secretory products, most of which are produced after exposure to various stimuli. 28 When activated by a variety of agonists, monocytes and macrophages produce proinfl ammatory cytokines (e.g. interleukin [IL] - 1, IL - 6, tumor necrosis factor - alpha [TNF α ]), anti - infl ammatory cytokines (e.g. IL - 10), growth factors (e.g. granulocyte - monocyte colony - stimulating factor, granulocyte colony stimulating factor, platelet - derived growth factor, and transforming growth factor β ), various enzymes (e.g. collagenase, elastase, acid hydroliases, and plasminogen activator), alternative and classic complement pathway factors, and prostaglandins and lipoxygenase products.
