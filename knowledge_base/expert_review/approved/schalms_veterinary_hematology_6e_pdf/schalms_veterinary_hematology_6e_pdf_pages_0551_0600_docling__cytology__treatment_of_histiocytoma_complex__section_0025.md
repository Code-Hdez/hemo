# Treatment of Histiocytoma Complex

## Treatment of Histiocytoma Complex

Solitary histiocytomas are either surgically removed or undergo spontaneous regression. Cutaneous LCH and systemic LCH are refractory to therapeutic intervention. Treatment with CCNU (Lomustine), which is commonly used to treat HS, has not been effective in LCH. Corticosteroids are contraindicated for reasons alluded to above. Spontaneous remission is possible in cutaneous LCH and this occurs in about half of the cases, unless lymph node involvement occurs. In one published report, response of cutaneous LCH to immunomodulatory therapy with griseofulvin was reported. 20 Others have tried immunomodulation with levamisole without success in cutaneous LCH.
