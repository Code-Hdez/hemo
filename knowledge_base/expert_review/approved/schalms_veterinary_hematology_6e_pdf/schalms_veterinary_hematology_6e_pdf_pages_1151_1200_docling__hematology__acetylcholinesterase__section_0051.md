# Acetylcholinesterase

## Acetylcholinesterase

Acetylcholinestease (ACH) is another enzymatic marker that specically identifi es mature megakaryocytes and platelets from dogs, cats, and rats. 43,48 In addition, ACH is found also within human and murine erythroid precursors. 55 Specimens for cytochemical staining are incubated in a medium containing acetylthiocholine iodide as substrate. The subsequent reaction product is brown.

Acetylcholinestease staining was helpful in the identifi cation of megakaryocytic precursors in canine cases of megakaryoblastic leukemia and essential or primary thrombocythemia. 18,40
