# X - LINKED FACTOR DEFICIENCIES: HEMOPHILIA A AND B

## X - LINKED FACTOR DEFICIENCIES: HEMOPHILIA A AND B

Hemophilia A (OMIM 306700) and hemophilia B (OMIM 306900) are distinct X - linked bleeding disorders caused by functional or quantitative defi ciencies of FVIII and FIX, respectively. 11 Although both genes map to the long arm of the X chromosome, they are inherited independently. Factor IX (a serine protease enzyme) and FVIII (its coenzyme) play critical roles in the amplifi cation phase of coagulation by participating in formation of the ' tenase ' enzyme complex (see Chapter 84 ).
