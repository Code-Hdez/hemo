# Cluster of Differentiation ( CD )34

## Cluster of Differentiation ( CD )34

CD34 is a cell surface glycoprotein that has traditionally been used in identifi cation and purifi cation of HSCs and progenitor cells. 1 This marker appears to be highly conserved among mammalian species. Experimental evidence suggests that CD34 may be involved in cell adhesion of hematopoietic cells to stromal cells in the bone marrow microenvironment. 10 More recently, however, CD34 - negative HSCs called side population (SP) cells were identifi ed. SP cells are thought to be some of the most primitive HSCs because of their high proliferative potential and extreme effi ciency at homing to sites of hematopoiesis when injected into recipient mice. 19 CD34 expression on HSCs may thus be related to the degree of activation of these cells, with CD34 negative cells being the most primitive and quiescent. 1
