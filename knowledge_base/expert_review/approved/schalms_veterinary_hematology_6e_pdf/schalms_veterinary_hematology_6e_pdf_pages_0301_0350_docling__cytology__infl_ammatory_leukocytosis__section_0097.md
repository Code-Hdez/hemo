## Infl ammatory Leukocytosis

The leukogram is an excellent method by which to monitor infl ammation in the dog. Infections caused by bacteria, rickettsia, viruses, fungi, or parasites cause alterations in the leukogram. Immune - mediated diseases, tissue necrosis, and neoplasia also elicit infl ammatory reactions. Because neutrophils respond within hours to various chemotactic stimuli during infl ammation, the time of phlebotomy relative to initial infl ammatory stimulus is important in interpretation of the leukogram.
