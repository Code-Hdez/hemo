# Hemosiderin

## Hemosiderin

Hemosiderin is a granular, iron-containing pigment resulting from the phagocytic breakdown of heme protein (Fig. 20.6). It can accumulate not only within hepatocytes during disease states, such as hemolytic anemia and chronic inflammatory conditions, but also with repeated blood transfusions and iron injections. It is also commonly seen within macrophages with or without phagocytized erythrocytes. Hemosiderin color can range from golden-brown to blue-black in cytology stains, whereas it has a more consistent golden-brown tint on H&amp;E histological sections. Should verification of hemosiderin pigment be needed, the special Prussian blue stain is used. ␣
