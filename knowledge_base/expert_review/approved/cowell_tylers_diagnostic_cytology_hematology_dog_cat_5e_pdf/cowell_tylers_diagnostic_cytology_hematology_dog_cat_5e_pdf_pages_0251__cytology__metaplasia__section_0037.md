# Metaplasia

## Metaplasia

Metaplasia is an adaptive response of epithelial cells to chronic irritation. 39 Replacement of normal pulmonary epithelial cells of the trachea, bronchi, and bronchioles with stratified squamous epithelium (i.e., squamous metaplasia) is an example of pulmonary metaplasia. 39 These metaplastic cells mimic maturing squamous epithelium and must not be confused with neoplasia. ␣
