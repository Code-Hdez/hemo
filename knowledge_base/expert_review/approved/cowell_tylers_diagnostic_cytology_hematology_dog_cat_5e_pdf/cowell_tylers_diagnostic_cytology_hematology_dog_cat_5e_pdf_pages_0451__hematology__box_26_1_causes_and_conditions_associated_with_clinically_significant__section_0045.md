# BOX 26.1 Causes and Conditions Associated With Clinically Significant Alteration in Peripheral Blood Platelet Count

## BOX 26.1 Causes and Conditions Associated With Clinically Significant Alteration in Peripheral Blood Platelet Count


Immune-mediated thrombocytopenia

Drug induced (usually an immune-mediated process [e.g., certain antibiotics or heparin therapy]) ␣


Disseminated intravascular coagulation

Major vessel thrombosis

Acute severe hemorrhage (e.g., some cases of Brodifacoum toxicity)

␣


Splenic disease (e.g., splenomegaly, splenic torsion, severe splenic congestion, and splenic neoplasia)

Anaphylaxis, endotoxemia (sequestration in microvasculature)

Certain drugs (e.g., barbiturates)

Addison disease

␣


Pancytopenic syndrome (e.g., chronic Ehrlichia spp. infection, Fanconi syndrome, estrogen toxicity)

Marrow infiltration (leukemia, myelofibrosis, myelophthisic conditions)

Cyclic hematopoiesis

Chemotherapeutic cytotoxic and cytostatic drugs

Severe nutritional deficiencies

␣
