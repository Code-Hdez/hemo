# Iris and Ciliary Body

## Iris and Ciliary Body

Space-occupying masses on the anterior uvea may be an indication for cytological examination of fine-needle aspirates. Direct aspiration of the iris nodule is performed with the animal under general anesthesia, as described in the previous section on aqueous humor.
