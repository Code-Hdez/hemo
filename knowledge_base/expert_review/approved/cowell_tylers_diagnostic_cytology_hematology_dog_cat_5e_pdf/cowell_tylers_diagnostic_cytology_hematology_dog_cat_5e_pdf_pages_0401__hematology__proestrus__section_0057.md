# Proestrus

## Proestrus

As ovarian follicles mature and serum concentrations of estrogen (estradiol) increase, the vaginal epithelium proliferates, and erythrocytes pass through uterine capillaries. These changes result in the typical appearance of vaginal cytological preparations made during proestrus (Fig. 25.10). Cytological specimens obtained in early and midproestrus are characterized by a mixture of epithelial cells, including parabasal, small and large intermediate cells, and superficial cells, along with variable numbers of neutrophils and erythrocytes. Basophilic mucous may be present in the background. As proestrus continues and the vaginal epithelium thickens, neutrophils are no longer able to traverse the vaginal wall, so their numbers decrease. Progressively higher percentages of large intermediate and superficial cells are present, with fewer parabasal and small intermediate cells. After serum estrogen levels peak in late proestrus, greater than 80% of epithelial cells are superficial, and neutrophils are absent. This is identical to the vaginal cytology during estrus. 9 Erythrocytes may be abundant or absent throughout proestrus. Bacteria, both free and on the surface of epithelial cells, are often present in large numbers. The mean duration of proestrus in mature dogs is 9 days, although a range of 2 to 17 days has been reported. 2 ␣
