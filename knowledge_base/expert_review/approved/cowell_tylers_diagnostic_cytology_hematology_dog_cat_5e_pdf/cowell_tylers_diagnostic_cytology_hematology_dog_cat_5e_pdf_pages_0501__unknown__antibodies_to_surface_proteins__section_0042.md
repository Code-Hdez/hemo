# Antibodies to Surface Proteins

## Antibodies to Surface Proteins

Commercially available antibodies to cell surface proteins are available for tests in dogs and cats, and many are prelabeled with fluorescent dyes. When cells bound by these antibodies pass through the laser in the instrument, the fluorescent molecules are excited to a higher energy state and emit light at a specific wavelength that is measured by the instrument. Therefore cells that express a given surface marker are detected as a fluorescent signal. The use of different fluorescent dyes allows measurement of multiple surface proteins simultaneously within a cell population. ␣
