# Alterations of WBC Numbers

## Alterations of WBC Numbers

The WBC count can be roughly classified as high, normal, or low by scanning the blood smear monolayer and the feathered edge with the 10× or 20× objective. The percentages of each WBC type can be estimated by scanning the smear or determined by a 100-differential nucleated cell count.
