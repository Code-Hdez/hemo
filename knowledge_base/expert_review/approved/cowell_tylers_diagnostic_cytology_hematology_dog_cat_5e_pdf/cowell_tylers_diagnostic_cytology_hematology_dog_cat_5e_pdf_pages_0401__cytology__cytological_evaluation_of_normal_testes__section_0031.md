# Cytological Evaluation of Normal Testes

## Cytological Evaluation of Normal Testes

The cellularity of a sample obtained from an aspirate or core of a normal testicle may be quite variable, depending on the aggressiveness of sampling. Cellularity is typically moderate to high, composed of a pleomorphic population of cells consisting of spermatozoa and their precursors, scattered Sertoli cells, and, rarely, Leydig (interstitial) cells, typically seen in loosely cohesive clusters or as single cells (Figs. 24.16 and 24.17). The material obtained from normal testicles is often contaminated with blood, and many bare nuclei and cytoplasmic fragments may be observed in the background. 9 Morphology of the testicular cell types is similar in dogs and cats. 47 Because multinucleate and large cells with prominent nucleoli may be apparent, care should be taken not to diagnose malignant neoplasia. In addition, some of the spermatogenic precursors may appear similar to large lymphocytes, and a misdiagnosis of lymphoma is possible (Fig. 24.18).
