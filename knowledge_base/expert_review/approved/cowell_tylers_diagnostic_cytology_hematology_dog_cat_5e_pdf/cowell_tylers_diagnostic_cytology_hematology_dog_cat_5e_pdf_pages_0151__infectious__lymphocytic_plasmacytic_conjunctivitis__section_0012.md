# Lymphocytic -Plasmacytic Conjunctivitis

## Lymphocytic -Plasmacytic Conjunctivitis

Conjunctivitis in which lymphoid cells predominate is less common than purulent conjunctivitis. Lymphocytic-plasmacytic conjunctivitis occurs in allergic and chronic infectious conjunctivitis (Fig. 9.12). Follicular conjunctivitis yields cells typical of reactive lymphoid hyperplasia (see the section 'Nictitating Membrane'). ␣
