# ARTHROCENTESIS

## ARTHROCENTESIS

In the verification, localization, diagnosis, and management of arthritis, synovial fluid examination is a key component of an initial medical database and includes clinical history, physical examination, radiographs, complete blood cell count, biochemical profile, and urinalysis. See Boxes 12.1 and 12.2 for indications and contraindications for arthrocentesis. ␣
