(A) Low-power magnification from draining cutaneous lesion in a cat. Three large dark-blue spherules are found with marked pyogranulomatous inflammation. The spherules are thick walled and usually keep a three-dimensional shape on cytology smears. Because of this, it is usually not possible to see the spherules sharply in focus in the same plane of focus as the inflammatory cells.
