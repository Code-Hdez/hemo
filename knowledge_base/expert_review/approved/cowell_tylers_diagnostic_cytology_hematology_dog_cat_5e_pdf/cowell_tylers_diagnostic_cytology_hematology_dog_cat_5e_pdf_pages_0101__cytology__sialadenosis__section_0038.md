# Sialadenosis

## Sialadenosis

Idiopathic unilateral or bilateral enlargement of the mandibular salivary glands (sialadenosis) associated with clinical signs of hypersalivation, retching/gagging or gulping, and vomiting has been reported in both dogs and cats. 29-32 Aspirates from affected glands have shown normal salivary epithelium, and histopathological evaluation has revealed normal salivary tissue with no evidence of inflammation or necrosis. These animals typically respond to oral phenobarbital therapy, suggesting a neurogenic cause. 29,30,32 Hypersalivation with salivary gland enlargement caused by acinar hyperplasia is reported as a common clinical finding associated with spirocercosis. 33 ␣
