# Idiopathic (Type I) Polyarthritis

## Idiopathic (Type I) Polyarthritis

Idiopathic, or immune-mediated, polyarthritis is diagnosed by exclusion of other possible causes or specific disease and breed associations. Among canine immune-mediated arthritides, idiopathic type I polyarthritis is the most common. 49 Dogs frequently present with stiffness, pyrexia, lymphadenopathy, and inappetence.
