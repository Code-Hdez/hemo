# Chondrosarcoma

## Chondrosarcoma

Chondrosarcomas occur most commonly in young dogs. Fine-needle aspirates typically yield moderately abundant amorphous, homogeneous, eosinophilic to magenta matrix compatible with cartilage. Cellularity may be minimal, and cells may stain poorly, especially in the presence of thick matrix. One to several chondrocytes embedded within cartilaginous lacunae is a unique feature of this neoplasm. Chondrocytes are round to oval, with round to oval nuclei, occasional binucleate cells, stippled to irregularly condensed chromatin, multiple nucleoli, and abundant variably vacuolated basophilic cytoplasm. There may be moderate to marked anisokaryosis, with moderate to marked variation in the N:C ratio. 42 ␣
