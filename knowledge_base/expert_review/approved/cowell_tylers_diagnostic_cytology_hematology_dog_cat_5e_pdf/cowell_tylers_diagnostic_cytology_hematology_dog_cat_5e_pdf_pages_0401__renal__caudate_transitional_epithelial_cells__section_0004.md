# Caudate Transitional Epithelial Cells

## Caudate Transitional Epithelial Cells

Smaller transitional epithelial cells with caudate morphology specifically line the renal pelves (Fig. 23.10). These cells are rarely observed in the urine sediment. They are an abnormal finding that may be seen in patients with renal pelves disease, for example, pyelonephritis, renal pelvic calculi. ␣
