# Causes of Dysplasia

## Causes of Dysplasia

- Neoplasia (hematopoietic neoplasia, nonhematopoietic neoplasia)
- Severe hyperplasia within a lineage
- Toxic insult; drug administration (some chemotherapy)
- Nutritional deficiency (folate, cobalamin)
- Congenital anomaly (macrocytosis in Poodle, congenital dyserythropoiesis in English Springer Spaniel)
- Infectious (FeLV, vector-borne infection)
- Necrosis/inflammation/fibrosis in bone marrow

FeLV, feline leukemia virus.
