# SUMMARY

## SUMMARY

Flow cytometry is becoming more widely available to practicing veterinarians through university and private reference laboratories. In addition to its common applications, such as in CBC, flow cytometry can be used to support a diagnosis of lymphoma and leukemia and can provide important prognostic information. Flow cytometry can also be helpful in supporting a diagnosis of IMHA or idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura (ITP). Additional applications will likely develop from current research.
