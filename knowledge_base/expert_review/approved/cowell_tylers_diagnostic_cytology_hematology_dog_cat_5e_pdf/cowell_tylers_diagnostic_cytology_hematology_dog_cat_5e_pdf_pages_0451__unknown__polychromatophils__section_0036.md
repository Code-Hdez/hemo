# Polychromatophils

## Polychromatophils

Polychromatophils are immature RBCs that are usually larger and more basophilic than mature cells (see Fig. 26.26). Polychromatophils occur in up to 0.5% and 1% of the circulating RBC pool of healthy cats and dogs, respectively. Higher proportions of polychromatophils suggest increased erythropoiesis. ␣
