# Juvenile-Onset Polyarthritis of Akitas

## Juvenile-Onset Polyarthritis of Akitas

Clinical signs of polyarthritis syndrome in Akitas typically appear before age 8 months and include neutrophilic arthritis, cyclical pain, generalized lymphadenopathy, and nonregenerative anemia. Rarely dogs have concurrent meningitis, have positive ANA test results, or are positive for rheumatoid factors. 74 It is speculated that this may be a canine overlap syndrome, in which patients have concurrent SLE and rheumatoid arthritis. ␣
