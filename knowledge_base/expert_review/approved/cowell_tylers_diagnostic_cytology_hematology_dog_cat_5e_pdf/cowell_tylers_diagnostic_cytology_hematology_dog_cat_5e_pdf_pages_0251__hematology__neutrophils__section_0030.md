# Neutrophils

## Neutrophils

In TW/BAL samples, neutrophils look like peripheral blood neutrophils (Figs. 16.11 and 16.12), although degenerative changes may be present. Increased numbers of neutrophils indicate inflammation; see the discussion on inflammation later in this chapter. ␣

Fig. 16.8 Tracheal wash or bronchoalveolar lavage from a dog. Mucus, scattered neutrophils, and a large number of eosinophils are shown. Some extracellular bacterial rods, probably from oropharyngeal contamination, are also present (Wright stain, original magnification 330×).
