# Myelolipoma

## Myelolipoma

Myelolipomas are very uncommon in dogs but have been reported. 9 These tumors are benign and often originate in the cortex but may extend into the medulla. Expected cytological findings would include lipid and mature adipocytes admixed with hematopoietic cells and macrophages containing hemosiderin. 9 ␣
