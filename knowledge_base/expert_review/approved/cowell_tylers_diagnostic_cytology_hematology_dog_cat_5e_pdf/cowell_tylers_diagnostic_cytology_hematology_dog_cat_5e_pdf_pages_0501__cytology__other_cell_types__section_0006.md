# OTHER CELL TYPES

## OTHER CELL TYPES

Additional cell types expected in bone marrow include lymphocytes, plasma cells, macrophages, mast cells, and stromal elements. These cell types are typically seen to some degree in normal bone marrow, but under certain disease states each of these cell types may become more prominent or increased in number (Box 27.12). With panhypoplasia of bone marrow, lack of hematopoietic cells often leads to apparent increased proportions of these background cellular elements (Fig. 27.27). With routine cytological bone marrow assessment, lymphocytes, plasma cells, and macrophages are typically counted as separate categories in parallel with the differential cell count of the myeloid and erythroid cells but are not included in the M:E ratio.
