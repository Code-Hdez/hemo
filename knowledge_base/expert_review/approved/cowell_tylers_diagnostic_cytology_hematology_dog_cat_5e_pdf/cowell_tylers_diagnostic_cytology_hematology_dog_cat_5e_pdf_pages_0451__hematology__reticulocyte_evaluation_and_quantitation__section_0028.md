# Reticulocyte Evaluation and Quantitation

## Reticulocyte Evaluation and Quantitation

One of a few vital stains that can be used to distinguish immature from mature erythrocytes on blood films is NMB. The stain combines with polyribosomes retained in immature RBCs (reticulocytes), which are then recognized as dark-blue granules or 'reticulum' within the RBC cytoplasm. Mature RBCs lack cytoplasmic ribosomes and stain uniformly pale blue with NMB.
