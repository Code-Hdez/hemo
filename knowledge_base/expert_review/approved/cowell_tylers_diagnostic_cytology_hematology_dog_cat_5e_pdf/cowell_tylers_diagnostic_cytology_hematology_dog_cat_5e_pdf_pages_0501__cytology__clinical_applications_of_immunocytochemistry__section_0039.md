# CLINICAL APPLICATIONS OF IMMUNOCYTOCHEMISTRY

## CLINICAL APPLICATIONS OF IMMUNOCYTOCHEMISTRY

One of the advantages of cytology over biopsy is the relatively low cost and rapid turnaround time, both of which will increase with the use of ICC. Therefore ICC is not necessary for every sample but should be reserved for very specific purposes. Transformed cells do not elaborate a distinct 'neoplastic' marker, so the diagnosis of malignancy needs to be made on standard cytology specimens before ICC. 17 Only after a diagnosis of neoplasia is made from the Romanowsky-stained cytology specimen, ICC may be able to illuminate a more specific diagnosis. Ideally, the slides used for ICC originate from the same aspirate or at least from the same location as the original cytology.
