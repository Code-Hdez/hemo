# Epithelial Cells

## Epithelial Cells

Epithelial cells in urine vary in size, depending on the origin. They are larger in the lower urinary tract versus in the ureters, renal pelves, and renal tubules. It is routine to see fewer than 5 epithelial cells/10× lowpower field (lpf) in normal urine samples. At times, it may be challenging to distinguish the different types of epithelial cells.
