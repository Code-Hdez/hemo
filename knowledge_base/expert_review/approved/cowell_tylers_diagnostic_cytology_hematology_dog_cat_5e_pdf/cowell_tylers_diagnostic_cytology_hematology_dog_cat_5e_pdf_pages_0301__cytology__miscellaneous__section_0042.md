# Miscellaneous

## Miscellaneous

Although rarely reported in cats and dogs, ectopic splenic tissue can be found in the pancreas, and this is an incidental finding that could be mistaken for pancreatic neoplasia on imaging. These will contain splenic stromal tissue, lymphoid cells, and hemosiderin-laden macrophages. 48 ␣
