# Basal Cells

## Basal Cells

Basal cells give rise to all epithelial cell types observed in a vaginal smear. They are small cells with round nuclei and a high nucleusto-cytoplasm (N:C) ratio and are rarely observed in vaginal smears (Fig. 25.4, A). ␣
