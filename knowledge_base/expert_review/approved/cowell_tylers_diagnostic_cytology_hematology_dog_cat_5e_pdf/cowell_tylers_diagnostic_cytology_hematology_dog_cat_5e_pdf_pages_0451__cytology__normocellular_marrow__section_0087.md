# Normocellular Marrow

## Normocellular Marrow

A normocellular marrow (approximately 50% hematopoietic cells; see Fig. 27.9) may be appropriate/normal or inappropriate/abnormal and should be interpreted in the context of the patient's age, the amount of peripheral demand for hematopoiesis, the M:E ratio, and the presence or absence of neoplasia or inflammation/infection on marrow assessment. Some marrows may appear normocellular with low magnification on cytology, but on closer inspection the cellularity within the unit particles is actually composed of increased numbers of plasma cells and macrophages with decreased numbers of hematopoietic precursors. ␣
