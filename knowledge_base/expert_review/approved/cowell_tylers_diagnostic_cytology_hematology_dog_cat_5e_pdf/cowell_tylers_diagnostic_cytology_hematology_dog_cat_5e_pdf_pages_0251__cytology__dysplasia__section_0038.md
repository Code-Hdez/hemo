# Dysplasia

## Dysplasia

Dysplasia is, by definition, a nonneoplastic change; however, severely dysplastic changes are sometimes referred to as carcinoma in situ. Dysplastic changes include variation in cell size and shape, darkerstaining cells, and increased nucleus-to-cytoplasm (N:C) ratio and numbers of immature cells. These changes can be difficult to differentiate from neoplasia and may progress to neoplasia. 52 ␣
