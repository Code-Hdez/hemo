# ANEMIA: DIAGNOSIS AND CLASSIFICATION

## ANEMIA: DIAGNOSIS AND CLASSIFICATION

Anemia is an absolute decrease in the Hct, Hb concentration, and/or RBC count. Relative anemia may occur when the plasma volume is expanded (e.g., excessive parenteral fluid administration, pregnancy , neonates) or when blood specimens are improperly obtained from intravenous fluid lines (pseudoanemia).
