# DIFFERENTIAL FEATURES OF ANEMIAS CAUSED BY EXTERNAL AND INTERNAL HEMORRHAGE

## III. DIFFERENTIAL FEATURES OF ANEMIAS CAUSED BY EXTERNAL AND INTERNAL HEMORRHAGE

- A. External blood loss, including gastrointestinal bleeding, prevents reutilization of certain components such as iron and plasma protein. These may be reabsorbed and recycled following internal hemorrhage.

- B. Internal hemorrhage may be less severe and more intensely regenerative. Following internal hemorrhage, some erythrocytes are reabsorbed by lymphatic vessels (autotransfusion), particularly when hemorrhage occurs into body cavities. Remaining erythrocytes are lysed or phagocytosed. The iron and amino acids from catabolized proteins are recycled.
