# Means of reticulocyte enumeration

## D. Means of reticulocyte enumeration

1. Reticulocyte percentage. Reticulocytes are counted in a NMB-stained blood smear and are expressed as the percentage of the total erythrocyte population. This parameter can overestimate the bone marrow response because:
- a. Reticulocytes released from the bone marrow into the blood of anemic animals are mixed with fewer mature erythrocytes. Thus, a higher relative percentage of reticulocytes results.
- b. Larger, more immature reticulocytes are released earlier (shift reticulocytes) in response to anemia. These reticulocytes persist longer in the blood because it takes them more time to mature. This results in a higher percentage of reticulocytes.
2. Corrected reticulocyte percentage
- a. Formula: Observed reticulocyte percentage patient's Hct percentage × ( ÷ ' ' normal Hct percentage )
