# INTRODUCTION

## INTRODUCTION

Cytology is a rapid, simple, and economical diagnostic technique that usually poses minimal risk to the patient. In many cases, a definitive cytologic diagnosis can be made. However, even when a specific diagnosis cannot be rendered, the information provided by cytology (e.g., inflammation vs. neoplasia) is useful for choosing additional tests to make a definitive diagnosis (e.g., culture vs. histopathology).
